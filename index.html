Who keeps the air clean

The people keeping salon air clean are the owners themselves.

There's a lot of talk about clean air and doing good. But in a salon it's real work, and it falls on one person.

For a long time nobody could help. Not because they didn't care, but because there was no way to know it was happening. That's what BeautiFi changes.

  • They buy the ventilation.
  • They pay to put it in.
  • They pay to keep it running.
  • They pay for the electricity.
  • They run it every single day.
What changed

Running ventilation isn't just a cost. Now every hour counts.

You don't have to change a thing. You already own the ventilation and use it every day. BeautiFi simply records what you already do, and checks it.

Each hour that passes its checks becomes a Verified Mitigation Hour: a public, sealed record of source-capture operation that anyone can open and re-check.

The logic

When the work is recorded, people can see it.

When people can see it, they can support it.

And the people doing the work can finally be rewarded by the people who care.

How it works

Three steps, start to finish.

Operate

The salon runs its ventilation.

Sensors record each hour as it happens, not from a form filled out later.

Verify

Each hour is checked.

Five validation events per hour, tested against airflow physics, then sealed into a public record that can't be edited.

Settle

The salon is rewarded.

Sponsors retire verified hours, and that support goes straight to the operators who earned them.

Nothing on the record can be changed in secret, and every figure traces back to the evidence behind it.

For sponsors

Most claims ask to be believed. This one can be checked.

Your sponsorship retires verified mitigation hours from the public pool. Once they're retired, no one else can claim them, and your name is on the settlement.

Behind it sits the proof. Every hour is methodology-pinned and evidence-linked, open to independent reproduction. Your claim is never just your word.

A Beautiful Vision

Our first deployment, BeautiFi v1, equips beauty salons with ventilation and air-quality sensors, proves the clean-air work they do, and lets brands and ESG funds directly fund and retire that work with cryptographic proof.
